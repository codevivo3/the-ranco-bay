# Project boundaries

## Product priorities

- Accommodation is the primary commercial product and must be immediately understandable.
- The private beach is a primary differentiator and should receive clear, factual emphasis in later editorial work.
- The local guide is a core product feature, built from owner knowledge rather than generic destination listings.
- A private Guest Companion is planned for confirmed guests, but authentication and companion functionality are deferred.
- Photography, private dinners and intimate gatherings may be considered selectively later. They are not a primary product area, and this codebase must not become an events platform.

## Experience direction

- The approved direction is editorial and cinematic, with accommodation clarity preceding spectacle.
- Do not reproduce a Booking.com-style interface, fake urgency or generic luxury language.
- The voice should feel local, specific and owner-led.
- The final visual implementation will follow the approved Figma source in a separate phase.
- Animation technology will be selected only after the static implementation and interaction prototype.

## Locked foundation

- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, ESLint and next-intl
- Server Components by default
- Sanity as the future CMS, separated from application types
- Questrial for display and brand typography; Manrope for body and interface text
- Sand `#F0EDDA`, Walnut `#5F4433`, Lake `#244B74` and Lagoon `#13788A`
- Multilingual architecture from the beginning
- English, Italian, German and French as the initial locales, with English as the source/default language

Booking, maps, payments, authentication, analytics, cookie consent, production CMS content, final copy, final translations and deployment remain outside this phase.
